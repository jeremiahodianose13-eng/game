How to play

  1. Move with the stick on the left. Drag anywhere else to look around. On a keyboard use WASD, space to jump, and click to capture the mouse.
  2. Break the block in your crosshair with the pickaxe button (left click). Breaking a block sometimes drops a coin.
  3. Place the selected material with the block button (right click). Pick materials from the bar at the top.
  4. Talk to a villager when the Talk button appears. Each has a request, and a flagged plot where they want a house.
  5. A house needs at least 24 placed blocks, a door and 8 roof blocks on the villager's plot.
  6. Shop: spend coins to unlock new materials like marble, gold, ruby, emerald and crystal.
  7. Layouts: the game fits phones, tablets and laptops automatically. You can switch on the Home page or in the pause menu.
  8. Extras: push the stick all the way (or hold Shift) to sprint. Use the mouse wheel to change material. Fireflies come out at night. Take a photo of your build from the pause menu.
  9. Endless materials: every material you own never runs out. There are 120 different items to unlock, from blocks to beds, lamps, sofas and a piano.
  10. Doors and furniture: tap a door to open or close it. Tap lamps, the TV, the stove and the fireplace to switch them on and off, tap a bed after 6 PM (or use Sleep until morning in the pause menu) to sleep and wake up the next morning with a small rest bonus, and tap the piano to play.
  11. Talking: villagers chat in speech bubbles and say it out loud when you are close. Press Talk, then Chat, to hear more. You can turn voices off in the pause menu.
  12. Wide world: a 120 by 120 world with a village plaza, ten ready-made houses, roads, forests and hills. Walk far, the world follows you.
  13. Village: ready-made houses stand around the world with neighbours who give a small gift every day. Chickens, sheep, cows, dogs and cats roam about. Press Talk next to an animal to pet it.
  14. Controls: the crosshair shows the block name, and a gold outline shows where a block will land. Tap Blocks (or press Q) for the full material list. The pause menu has look sensitivity, haptics and left-handed mode.
  15. Account: create a username and password on the first screen. Your worlds are saved per account on this device. Log out from the Home page.
  16. Music: a calm score plays while you build and turns softer at night. Change the volume in the pause menu.
